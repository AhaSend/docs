A campaign goes out in four steps: a test to yourself, the checks on the campaign page, a send or schedule, and AhaSend's own review of the list and the content. This page explains each step, the statuses a campaign passes through, and what to do when it is held, paused or needs a change.

## Test Sends

Click **Send Test** on the campaign page and enter 1 to 5 addresses. Each address gets the email as a recipient would, with sample values in place of the contact fields. The links in a test lead to a contact that does not exist, so a click on the unsubscribe link in a test unsubscribes nobody.

Rules for tests:

- A test needs a complete campaign, with one exception: the unsubscribe link may still be missing. Any other open issue blocks the test as well as the send, so the test is a real rehearsal.
- Your account can send 50 test emails per day (UTC), counted per address, from the dashboard and the API together.
- Test emails count against your [daily marketing limit](/campaigns/marketing-limit), but not against your plan's monthly volume.

## Issues

The campaign page lists what is missing before the campaign can go out. The same list comes back as `issues` from the [API](/api-reference/campaigns/get-campaign).

| Issue | What to fix |
|---|---|
| Sender missing | Set a sender on a verified domain that is not paused |
| Lists missing | Choose at least one list that still exists |
| Subject or preview text missing | Fill them in |
| Design missing | Open the editor and make a design; an imported HTML design needs HTML |
| Text broken | The subject, preview text or design has template syntax that cannot be rendered, for example an unclosed `{{` |
| Unsubscribe link missing | Put `{{ unsubscribe_url }}` back in the design |
| Text unchecked | The campaign could not be checked right now; nothing is known to be wrong. Check again in a moment |

## Send Now or Schedule

**Schedule & Send** sends the campaign now or at a date and time you choose. A scheduled campaign shows your daily marketing limit, so you can see whether it fits in one day.

Some sends are refused on the spot, with a message that says why: a send time less than a minute ahead, a sender domain that is not verified or is paused, and lists on which no contact can receive the campaign.

Changed your mind? **Unschedule** a scheduled campaign and it returns to draft, where you can edit it and send it again. Once a campaign is sending, it cannot go back to draft; to send the same email again, duplicate it.

## Review

AhaSend's marketing infrastructure is shared by the campaigns of all customers, so every campaign is checked before it goes out. The check protects your delivery from other senders' bad lists, and theirs from yours.

**The list.** Every address is checked: is it well-formed, does its domain accept mail, is the domain on AhaSend's list of known bad domains, and, where possible, does the mailbox exist. Addresses that cannot receive mail are left out of the send; addresses the check could not reach keep their status and still get the campaign. Addresses on catch-all domains, which accept mail for any address, cannot be confirmed and are shown as Catch-all in the list check on the report, next to Risky; they still get the campaign. A list with a high bounce risk, or a large list that is mostly catch-all addresses, is held for review before anything is sent.

**The content.** A language model that AhaSend fine-tuned and runs on its own servers in European datacenters helps catch campaigns that look like spam, phishing or cold outreach. AhaSend does not accept cold outreach: email to people who did not ask for it. See the [terms of service](https://ahasend.com/terms).

A campaign that passes continues to its send time. A campaign that is held shows the status **waiting for review** and the reason on its page. A person looks at it; when the review is done, the campaign continues, or it is canceled with the reason on its page. Contact [support](mailto:support@ahasend.com) if you want to discuss a held campaign.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Being prepared. Only a draft can be edited, tested, sent or deleted. |
| Validating | Scheduled; the lists are being checked. Can be unscheduled. |
| Waiting for review | Held for a review of the list or the content. Cannot be unscheduled. |
| Scheduled | Accepted, waiting for its send time. Can be unscheduled. |
| Sending | Going out now. |
| Paused | Sending stopped for now; the page says why. |
| Sent | Every message was sent. The report is complete. |
| Canceled | Stopped before it finished; the page says why. |
| Failed | Could not be sent; the page says why. |

## The Daily Marketing Limit

Campaigns do not send faster than your account's [daily marketing limit](/campaigns/marketing-limit). When the limit is used up, the campaign waits and continues when capacity comes back; a campaign larger than the limit continues over the next days. The report shows an estimated finish and, while the campaign waits, the time it continues. The limit starts low for new accounts and grows with your plan, account age, volume and a low spam rate; support can raise it.

## After the Send

Open the report for delivered, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, complaints and bounces. See [reports](/campaigns/reports). Unsubscribes and spam complaints that mailbox providers report back are processed for you: the contact leaves that list and gets no more campaigns from it.
