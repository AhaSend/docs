---
title: "List Sub-Accounts via the API"
sidebarTitle: "List Sub-Accounts"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml GET /v2/accounts/{account_id}/sub-accounts"
description: "List all active AhaSend sub-accounts under a parent account with cursor pagination. Soft-deleted sub-accounts are excluded from the results."
---


The AhaSend API returns a cursor-paginated list of sub-accounts under the parent account. Soft-deleted sub-accounts are not included; usage billed for sub-accounts deleted during the current period is reported by the usage endpoint's `removed_sub_accounts` aggregate.

<Info>
  **Platform Partner feature:** Sub-Accounts is part of our Platform Partner capabilities and is currently in early access. [Contact us](https://ahasend.com/contact) to enable it on your account.
</Info>
