---
title: "Unpause a Sub-Account Domain via the API"
sidebarTitle: "Unpause Sub-Account Domain"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml POST /v2/accounts/{account_id}/sub-accounts/{sub_account_id}/domains/{domain}/unpause"
description: "Lift the pause on a sending domain of an AhaSend sub-account from the parent account. The call is idempotent. Requires the sub-accounts:suspend scope."
---


The AhaSend API lifts the pause on a domain of a sub-account under the parent account.

<Info>
  **Platform Partner feature:** Sub-Accounts is part of our Platform Partner capabilities and is currently in early access. [Contact us](https://ahasend.com/contact) to enable it on your account.
</Info>

Fix the cause of the pause before you lift it. See [paused domains](/domains#why-is-sending-paused-on-my-domain).
