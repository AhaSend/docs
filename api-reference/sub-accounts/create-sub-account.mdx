description: "Create an isolated AhaSend sub-account under your parent account through the API, with a name, website and an optional monthly sending cap."
---


The AhaSend API creates a new sub-account under the parent account.

<Info>
  **Plan availability:** sub-accounts are included on Pro (up to 9) and Max (no limit). There is no charge per sub-account; the volume of all sub-accounts is pooled on the parent's plan. See [plans and features](/facts#plans-and-features).
</Info>
