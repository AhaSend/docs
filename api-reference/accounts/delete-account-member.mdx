sidebarTitle: "Remove Account Member"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml DELETE /v2/accounts/{account_id}/members/{user_id}"
description: "Remove a member from your AhaSend account through the API to revoke their access. You cannot remove yourself or the account owner this way."
---

Removes a member from the account

**Restrictions:**
- You cannot delete yourself
- You cannot delete the account owner
