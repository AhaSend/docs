sidebarTitle: "Create Route"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml POST /v2/accounts/{account_id}/routes"
description: "Create an AhaSend inbound email route that forwards messages sent to an address or wildcard on your domain to your webhook URL as JSON."
---

Creates a new route for inbound email routing. Authorization uses the
domain in `recipient`: the global write role or its matching
domain-specific write role is sufficient.
