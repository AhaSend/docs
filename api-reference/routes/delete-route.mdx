---
title: "Delete an Inbound Email Route via the API"
sidebarTitle: "Delete Route"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml DELETE /v2/accounts/{account_id}/routes/{route_id}"
description: "Permanently delete an AhaSend inbound email route so messages sent to its recipient pattern are no longer forwarded to your webhook URL."
---

Deletes a route. Access requires the global delete role or a delete role
matching the route's recipient domain.
