The AhaSend Segments API manages segments: saved rules over your contacts whose members stay up to date. A campaign can send to a segment or leave its members out. This page explains segments, their definitions and states, who a campaign to a segment reaches, and the rules that apply to all segment operations.

## Segments

A segment is a saved set of rules over the [contacts](/api-reference/contacts/overview) of your account. Its members are the contacts that match the rules. A rule can read:
- A field of the contact: `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, one of your own fields, or `created_at`, the time the contact was created.
- A list membership.
- The campaigns that the contact received, opened or clicked: one campaign, or any campaign since a time.
- The number of campaigns that the contact received, opened or clicked since a time.
- The month and day of one of your date fields, for birthdays and anniversaries.

You do not add members to a segment. AhaSend keeps the members up to date as your contacts, lists and campaigns change, usually within a minute. Rules about campaigns follow within an hour of a send, and relative times such as `30 days ago` are read again on a schedule.

Segment names do not have to be unique in an account. An account can have at most 100 segments.

A list, a campaign or a contact field that a segment uses in a rule cannot be deleted while the segment uses it. [Delete List](/api-reference/lists/delete-list) and [Delete Campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/delete-campaign) answer `409` with a message that names the segments, such as `Used by segments: A, B. Change those segments first.`

## Definitions

`definition` holds the rules of a segment:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "match": "all",
  "conditions": [
    { "field": "attributes.country", "op": "equal", "value": "DE" },
    { "field": "created_at", "op": "after", "value": "30 days ago" },
    { "field": "list", "op": "in", "value": "6f1c2a52-8f2e-4d8e-9a3b-1c2d3e4f5a6b" },
    {
      "match": "any",
      "conditions": [
        { "field": "campaign", "op": "opened", "value": "9a2e7c41-0b3d-4f5e-8a6b-7c8d9e0f1a2b" },
        { "field": "any_campaign", "op": "clicked", "value": "14 days ago" },
        { "field": "campaign_count", "op": "opened_at_least", "value": { "count": 3, "since": "90 days ago" } }
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

- `schema_version` is `1`.
- `match` is `all` when a contact must match all of the conditions, or `any` when one is enough.
- `conditions` holds at least one item. An item is a condition or a group. A group has its own `match` and `conditions`, and holds conditions only, so groups nest one level deep.
- A condition has a `field`, an operator in `op` and, for most operators, a `value`. For an operator that takes no value, such as `is_empty`, leave `value` out or send `null`.
- A definition has at most 50 conditions in all. At most 5 of them are `campaign`, `any_campaign` or `campaign_count` conditions.
- An unknown key is refused. A refused definition answers `400`, and the message names each problem by its path, such as `conditions[3].value`.

### Conditions

| `field` | Operators | `value` |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, a text field | `equal`, `not_equal`, `contains`, `not_contains`, `begins_with`, `not_begins_with`, `ends_with`, `not_ends_with`, `is_empty`, `is_not_empty` | A string |
| A number field | `equal`, `not_equal`, `less`, `less_or_equal`, `greater`, `greater_or_equal` | A number |
| `created_at`, a date field, a timestamp field | `equal`, `not_equal`, `before`, `before_or_on`, `after`, `after_or_on` | A date such as `2024-06-15`, or a relative time such as `today` or `30 days ago`. `created_at` and timestamp fields also take a time such as `2 hours from now`. |
| A boolean field | `is_true`, `is_false` | None |
| A date field | `month_is`, `anniversary_within` | A whole number. See [Birthdays and anniversaries](#birthdays-and-anniversaries). |
| `list` | `in`, `not_in` | A list ID |
| `campaign` | `received`, `not_received`, `opened`, `not_opened`, `clicked`, `not_clicked` | A campaign ID |
| `any_campaign` | `received`, `opened`, `clicked`, `not_opened`, `not_clicked` | A relative time such as `14 days ago` |
| `campaign_count` | `received_at_least`, `received_fewer_than`, `opened_at_least`, `opened_fewer_than`, `clicked_at_least`, `clicked_fewer_than` | A count object. See [Campaign counts](#campaign-counts). |

Your own fields are `attributes.<key>`, with the key of a contact field that you defined in the dashboard. A condition on a key that the account does not have is refused.

**Rules of the conditions:**
- Your date, number, timestamp and boolean fields also take `is_null` and `is_not_null`. `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `created_at` and your text fields do not.
- A negated operator, such as `not_equal` or `not_contains`, also matches a contact that has no value for the field.
- `list` with `in` means a `confirmed` membership of the list.
- `campaign` with `received` means that the campaign was delivered to the contact. `not_opened` and `not_clicked` match only contacts who received the campaign.
- `any_campaign` with `received`, `opened` or `clicked` matches a contact with at least one such event since the time. `not_opened` and `not_clicked` match a contact who received a campaign since the time and opened, or clicked, none since then.
- Opens and clicks by bots do not count.

### Campaign Counts

A `campaign_count` condition takes an object as its `value`, such as `{"count": 3, "since": "90 days ago"}`. It counts the campaigns sent since `since` that the contact received, opened or clicked.

- `count` is a whole number from 1 to 1,000, or from 2 for `received_fewer_than`.
- `since` is `M days ago`, `M weeks ago`, `M months ago` or `M years ago`, with M up to 365 days, 52 weeks, 12 months or 1 year. The window starts at the beginning of its day in the segment's time zone.
- A campaign counts from the time it started sending, whenever the contact received, opened or clicked it. Several opens or clicks of one campaign count once.
- `received_fewer_than`, `opened_fewer_than` and `clicked_fewer_than` match only a contact who received at least one campaign sent since then.
- A missing or unknown property of the object is refused.

### Birthdays and Anniversaries

Two operators on your date fields read only the month and day, so the year does not matter:
- `month_is` takes a month from `1` (January) to `12` (December).
- `anniversary_within` takes a number of days N from `0` to `365`. It matches when the month and day fall on today or on one of the next N days in the segment's time zone. `0` is today only.

A 29 February date counts as 28 February in a year without one. Both operators read a stored value that starts with a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, as the API and imports store it. Any other value never matches. The members of an `anniversary_within` segment move to the new day shortly after midnight in its time zone, usually within about half an hour.

### Time Zone

`timezone` is an IANA time zone, such as `Europe/Berlin`. Relative times, such as `today` or `30 days ago`, and days are read in this zone. Without one, the segment uses `UTC`. An unknown time zone is refused with `400`.

## States

| `state` | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `building` | AhaSend is finding the members of the current definition. A large account can take some minutes. |
| `ready` | The members follow the current definition. |
| `failed` | The members could not be found. `failure_reason` says why. |

- A new segment is `building`. An update that changes `definition` or `timezone` builds the members again, and the segment is `building` until that ends. An update of only `name` or `description` keeps the members and the state.
- While a segment is `building`, [List Segment Contacts](/api-reference/segments/get-segment-contacts) and [List a Contact's Segments](/api-reference/segments/get-contact-segments) may still answer by the previous rules.
- Any update of a `failed` segment, `{}` included, builds it again.
- A campaign that uses a segment, included or excluded, cannot be sent while the segment is `building` or `failed`. [Send Campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/send-campaign) refuses it with `400`, and the message names the segment.
- `last_evaluated_at` is the time the members were last checked against the whole account. It is `null` until the first build ends.

## Members and Reachable Contacts

The members of a segment are the contacts that match its rules, whatever their consent. `member_count` and [List Segment Contacts](/api-reference/segments/get-segment-contacts) include all of them.

A campaign to a segment reaches only the members who can get marketing email. A member is reachable when:
- The contact is `enabled`.
- The contact is not unsubscribed account-wide.
- Email validation did not find the contact invalid.
- The contact is `confirmed` on at least one list, or is on no list at all.

So a contact whose memberships are all `unsubscribed`, `complained` or `unconfirmed` is not reached. A contact on no list can be reached, which a campaign to lists cannot do.

[Get Segment](/api-reference/segments/get-segment) returns `marketable_count`, the members that a campaign to this segment alone would reach. It is `null` while the segment is not `ready`, and when the members cannot be counted within 10 seconds. The campaign checks its contacts again when it starts.

## Campaigns and Segments

A campaign sends to the segments in `segment_ids`, and leaves out the members of the segments in `exclude_segment_ids`. See [Audience](/api-reference/campaigns/overview#audience) for the rules of a campaign's lists and segments.

A segment send has no list to leave. A contact who unsubscribes from a segment send, or reports it as spam, is unsubscribed account-wide and gets no more marketing email from your account. The contact's `unsubscribed` becomes `true`. See [Contacts, lists and segments](/api-reference/contacts/overview#contacts-lists-and-segments).

A segment that a campaign still needs, as an included or an excluded segment, cannot be deleted. Campaigns in `draft`, `validating`, `scheduled`, `sending`, `paused` or `waiting_for_review` block the delete.

## Preview a Definition

[Preview Segment](/api-reference/segments/preview-segment) tries a definition without saving it. It returns `count`, the contacts that the definition matches now, whatever their consent, and up to 10 of them. It changes nothing.

## Pagination

List Segments and List a Contact's Segments return segments newest first. List Segment Contacts returns members by contact ID, lowest first. Each page gives `pagination.has_more`, `pagination.next_cursor` and `pagination.previous_cursor`. Send `next_cursor` as `after` for the next page, or `previous_cursor` as `before` for the previous page. You cannot send `after` and `before` together. `limit` is 1 to 100, and the default is 100.

## Conflicts

A segment write that meets another change answers `409` and changes nothing:

| Operation | Cause | What to do |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Create Segment | The account already has 100 segments. | Delete a segment, then retry. |
| Create Segment | A list or campaign that the definition names is being changed: `The segment is being updated. Try again in a minute.` | Retry later. |
| Update Segment, Delete Segment | The segment is being changed: `The segment is being updated. Try again in a minute.` | Retry later. |
| Delete Segment | A campaign still needs the segment: `This segment cannot be deleted while a campaign is still using it.` | Remove the segment from the campaign, or wait until the campaign is `sent`, `failed` or `canceled`. |
| Create Campaign, Update Campaign | A segment of the audience is being built again: `The campaign's audience is being updated. Try again in a minute.` | Retry later, with a new `Idempotency-Key` if you used one. |

Do not use the `message` to tell the cases apart.

## Scopes

| Scope | Grants |
|-------|--------|
| `segments:read` | List and view segments, preview a definition, and list the members of a segment and the segments of a contact |
| `segments:write` | Create and update segments |
| `segments:delete` | Delete segments |

Preview Segment, List Segment Contacts and List a Contact's Segments read contacts, so they also need `contacts:read`. See [Permission scopes](/api-reference/scopes#segments).

## Idempotency

Create Segment accepts an `Idempotency-Key` header. A `2xx` answer is stored for 24 hours, so a retry with the same key creates no second segment. Unlike most operations, a `4xx` answer is not stored: it releases the key, so a retry with the same key runs again, for example after you delete a segment. A request with the same key that is still in progress answers `409` with `Idempotent-Replayed: false` and a `Retry-After` header; retry with the same key after that delay. See [Idempotency](/api-reference/idempotency).
