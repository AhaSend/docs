openapi: "/openapi.yaml GET /v2/accounts/{account_id}/segments"
description: "List the segments of your AhaSend account, newest first, with the rules, the state and the member count of each segment and the time its members were checked."
---


The AhaSend API returns the account's segments, newest first. Access requires `segments:read`.

Each segment gives its `definition`, its `state` and `member_count`, the contacts that match its rules, whatever their consent. To read how many of them a campaign would reach, use [Get Segment](/api-reference/segments/get-segment). See [Members and reachable contacts](/api-reference/segments/overview#members-and-reachable-contacts).

Pages use cursors: send `after` or `before`, not both. See [Pagination](/api-reference/segments/overview#pagination).
