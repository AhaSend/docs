---
title: "Delete a Segment via the API"
sidebarTitle: "Delete Segment"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml DELETE /v2/accounts/{account_id}/segments/{segment_id}"
description: "Delete an AhaSend segment. Its contacts stay in your account, and a segment that an unfinished campaign sends to or excludes cannot be deleted."
---


The AhaSend API deletes one segment. Access requires `segments:delete`.

After the delete, the segment cannot be read, and its members are removed shortly after. The contacts themselves stay.

A campaign that still needs the segment, as an included or an excluded segment, blocks the delete with `409`. Campaigns in `draft`, `validating`, `scheduled`, `sending`, `paused` or `waiting_for_review` block it. Campaigns in `sent`, `failed` or `canceled` do not. A segment that is being changed also answers `409`. See [Conflicts](/api-reference/segments/overview#conflicts).

A second delete of the same segment answers `404`.
