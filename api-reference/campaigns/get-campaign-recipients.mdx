title: "List Campaign Recipients via the API"
sidebarTitle: "List Campaign Recipients"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml GET /v2/accounts/{account_id}/campaigns/{campaign_id}/recipients"
description: "List the recipients of an AhaSend marketing campaign in one status or with one bounce classification, with the source, attempts and bounce details of each."
---


The AhaSend API returns the recipients of a campaign, by contact ID, highest first. Access requires `campaigns:read`.

Send exactly one of `status` and `bounce_classification`:
- `status` is a key of `report.by_status` in [Get Campaign Stats](/api-reference/campaigns/get-campaign-stats), such as `Queued`, `Delivered` or `Administratively Bounced`.
- `bounce_classification` is a key of `report.by_bounce_classification`. `Unknown` counts bounces with no classification and cannot be listed.

Each recipient gives the address that the campaign sent to, the list or segment it was sent from, its status, its delivery attempts and, for a bounce, the classification, the reason and the message of the receiving server.

- A recipient sent to from a list has `list_name`, and `segment_id` and `segment_name` are `null`.
- A recipient sent to from a segment has `segment_id` and `segment_name`, the name of the segment at the send, and `list_name` is `null`.
- A contact reached through a list and a segment is recorded under the list. See [Audience](/api-reference/campaigns/overview#audience).

A page after the last recipient is empty and has no `previous_cursor`. To start again, read the first page without a cursor.
