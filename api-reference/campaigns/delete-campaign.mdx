title: "Delete a Campaign via the API"
sidebarTitle: "Delete Campaign"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml DELETE /v2/accounts/{account_id}/campaigns/{campaign_id}"
description: "Delete a draft AhaSend marketing campaign with its design and audience choices; lists and segments stay. Only a draft can be deleted, and a retry returns 404."
---


The AhaSend API deletes a draft campaign with its design and its audience choices. Access requires `campaigns:delete`.

A campaign that is not a draft answers `409`. A campaign that a segment names in a `campaign` condition also answers `409`, with a message that names the segments: `Used by segments: A, B. Change those segments first.` See [Conditions](/api-reference/segments/overview#conditions).

A second delete of the same campaign answers `404`. Deleting a campaign does not delete its lists, its segments or its contacts.
