---
title: "Delete a Campaign via the API"
sidebarTitle: "Delete Campaign"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml DELETE /v2/accounts/{account_id}/campaigns/{campaign_id}"
description: "Delete a draft AhaSend marketing campaign with its design and list choices; the lists stay. Only a draft can be deleted, and a second delete returns 404."
---


The AhaSend API deletes a draft campaign with its design and its list choices. Access requires `campaigns:delete`.

A campaign that is not a draft answers `409`. A second delete of the same campaign answers `404`. Deleting a campaign does not delete its lists or its contacts.
