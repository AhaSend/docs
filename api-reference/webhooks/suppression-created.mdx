AhaSend sends this event when a suppression is created for an email address, or when an existing suppression gets a new reason.

**Why suppressions are created**:
- A hard bounce from the email address (`origin: system`)
- Repeated soft bounces over time (`origin: system`)
- The recipient reports a transactional email as spam (reason `FBL Complaint Report`, `origin: recipient`)
- The recipient unsubscribes through the unsubscribe link of a transactional email (reason `Unsubscribed`, `origin: recipient`)
- You create a suppression through the API (`origin: api`). A request without a domain creates a suppression on each domain of your account, and sends one event for each.

An unsubscribe from a campaign email or a spam complaint about one creates no suppression. It sends [contact events](/api-reference/webhooks#contact-events) instead.

**Expiration**: A suppression after delivery errors expires after at most 30 days, depending on the error. A suppression after an unsubscribe or a spam complaint is effectively permanent. A suppression made through the API expires at the `expires_at` of its request. See [suppression durations](/bounces#suppression-duration).

**Impact**: Future emails to this address will be automatically suppressed until the suppression expires or is manually removed.

`data` is the suppression as it was right after the change, and `timestamp` is the time of the change. For a suppression that got a new reason, `data.created_at` stays the time the suppression was first created, so store suppressions by `data.recipient` and `data.sending_domain`.

**No event**:
- A repeat of an existing suppression, such as a second bounce or a second click on the same unsubscribe link, even when it moves the expiry later
- The removal of expired suppressions. `data.expires_at` already says when the suppression ends.

**Setting**: `on_suppression_created` (Suppression Created in the dashboard).

**When it's sent**: When a new suppression is added to your account, or when an existing suppression gets a new reason, for example when a recipient unsubscribes after a bounce.
