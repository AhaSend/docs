description: "The contact.complained webhook fires when an AhaSend contact reports a campaign email as spam. The payload holds the complaint report and the campaign."
---


AhaSend sends this event when a contact reports a campaign email as spam: a membership's subscription status becomes `complained`, or a complaint about a campaign sent to a segment is recorded for the campaign's recipient.

`data.complaint` is the spam complaint report, as on [`message.complained`](/api-reference/webhooks/message-complained), and `data.campaign_id` is the campaign of the reported email. For a membership, `data.previous_subscription_status` is the status before the change.

A recipient of a campaign sent to a segment who reports it as spam unsubscribes from all lists, so the event has `data.list` and `data.list_contact` set to `null`, and `data.segment_id` is the segment. This event is sent also when the contact is already unsubscribed, once for each campaign that the contact reports. `data.contact.unsubscribed` says whether the contact is unsubscribed from all lists after the complaint.

**No event**: A complaint about a membership that is already `complained`.

**Setting**: `on_contact_complained` (Contact Reported Spam in the dashboard).

**Requirements**:
- Your account has the marketing features. Contact events cannot be turned on without them.
- A webhook with this event on needs an API key with `contacts:read` and `lists:read` when you create or update it through the API. See [contact events](/api-reference/webhooks#contact-events).

**When it's sent**: When a spam complaint about a campaign email changes the contact's membership of the campaign's list, or is recorded for a recipient of a campaign sent to a segment.
