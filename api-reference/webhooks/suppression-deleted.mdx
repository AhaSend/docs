AhaSend sends this event when a suppression is deleted. Future emails to the address are attempted again.

**Why suppressions are deleted**:
- You delete a suppression through the API (`origin: api`). A request without a domain deletes the suppressions of the address on every domain of your account, and sends one event for each.
- You delete a suppression in the dashboard (`origin: dashboard`)
- AhaSend support deletes a suppression (`origin: system`)

`data` is the suppression as it was right before the delete, and `timestamp` is the time of the delete.

**No event**:
- [Wiping the suppressions](/api-reference/suppressions/wipe-suppressions) of your account or of a domain. After a wipe, read the list again with [List Suppressions](/api-reference/suppressions/get-suppressions).
- The removal of expired suppressions. `data.expires_at` of `suppression.created` already says when the suppression ends.

**Setting**: `on_suppression_deleted` (Suppression Deleted in the dashboard).

**When it's sent**: When a suppression is deleted.
