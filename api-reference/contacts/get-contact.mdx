---
title: "Get a Contact via the API"
sidebarTitle: "Get Contact"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml GET /v2/accounts/{account_id}/contacts/{id_or_email}"
description: "Retrieve one AhaSend contact by ID or email address, with its name, status, account-wide unsubscribe, attributes and validation result."
---


The AhaSend API returns one contact of the account. Access requires `contacts:read`.

`{id_or_email}` is the contact ID or its email address. A value in UUID form is read as the ID. Percent-encode an email address exactly once, so `user+tag@example.com` becomes `user%2Btag%40example.com`. The official SDKs encode it for you. See [Name a contact in the path](/api-reference/contacts/overview#name-a-contact-in-the-path).

The contact does not show its lists. To read them, use [List Contact's Lists](/api-reference/lists/get-contact-lists).
