description: "List the contacts in your AhaSend account, newest first. Filter by email address, status, account-wide subscription and creation time."
---


The AhaSend API returns the account's contacts, newest first. Access requires `contacts:read`.

**Filters:**
- `email` matches one email address exactly, after it is trimmed and changed to lowercase.
- `status` is `enabled`, `disabled` or `blocked`.
- `subscribed` filters by the account-wide unsubscribe of the contact. It does not look at list memberships.
- `from_time` and `to_time` limit the creation time. Both are RFC 3339 times, and both ends are included.

Pages use cursors: send `after` or `before`, not both. See [Pagination](/api-reference/contacts/overview#pagination). To list the contacts of one list, use [List Contacts on a List](/api-reference/lists/get-list-contacts).
