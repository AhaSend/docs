---
title: "List Campaign Templates via the API"
sidebarTitle: "List Templates"
openapi: "/openapi.yaml GET /v2/accounts/{account_id}/campaign-templates"
description: "List the campaign templates in your AhaSend account, newest first, with their names and editors. Transactional templates are not in this list."
---


The AhaSend API returns the account's campaign templates, newest first. Access requires `templates:read`.

Each campaign template is listed without its `content`. To read the content, use [Get Campaign Template](/api-reference/campaign-templates/get-campaign-template). Transactional templates are not in this list. To list them, use [List Templates](/api-reference/templates/get-templates).
