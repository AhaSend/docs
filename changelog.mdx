<Update label="July 2026">
#### New Features

- **CSV Contact Import**: You can now fill a contact list by uploading a CSV file — from the Contacts page, from an individual list, or directly in the Create List dialog so a new list is created and populated in one step. Contacts that already exist are updated and added to the list; new ones are created. Recognized columns are email, first name, last name, status, and any of your custom contact attributes.
- **Background Imports with Progress and Email Notification**: Files above roughly 500KB or 5,000 rows are processed in the background. The Contacts and list pages show a live progress banner with a Cancel button, and you receive an email when the import finishes reporting how many contacts were imported, how many joined the list, and how many rows were skipped. If an import fails, the email explains why whenever the cause is something you can fix — such as a malformed row and its line number, a missing email column, or an empty file — and re-uploading the same file is always safe.
- **Higher Hourly Sending Limits, Graduated by Plan**: Pro accounts now start at 1,000 messages per hour and step up to 5,000 and then 10,000 as you build your sending reputation and history with AhaSend; Max accounts start at 2,000 and step up to 20,000 and then 100,000 per hour. Legacy metered plans keep their existing 500 → 1,000 → 2,000 curve. Higher rate limits are possible after a manual review and approval, please [contact us](mailto:support@ahasend.com) if your sending patterns require higher rate limits.
- **Per-Service Cookie Controls**: "Manage preferences" now lets you allow or refuse individual services rather than only broad categories — PostHog analytics and UTM attribution under Analytics, Trackdesk affiliate tracking under Marketing. Refusing a category or service now actively deletes the cookies belonging to it, and withdrawing consent takes effect immediately in every open tab.

#### Changes

- **Contact email addresses are now case-insensitive.** Two spellings of the same address are now the same contact everywhere — on import, when adding a contact, in search, and in list segments. Existing contacts that differed only by capitalization have been merged into one, keeping the most restrictive state (unsubscribes, suppressions, and invalid-address verdicts always survive) and preserving names, custom attributes, list memberships, and engagement history. Previously, re-adding an address with different capitalization created a second contact that could bypass the original's unsubscribe.
- **Saved list segments with email rules were updated.** Segments filtering on an email address with "equals" or "does not equal" have had their values lowercased so they keep matching. "Contains", "begins with", and "ends with" rules were left exactly as you wrote them; they already ignore capitalization.
- **Campaigns now skip globally unsubscribed contacts.** They are excluded when a campaign is initialized, again before each batch is sent, and in pre-send list validation. The rejection reason shown on a campaign report changed from "disabled or invalid" to "unsubscribed, disabled, or invalid".
- **Creating a list with no filter now creates an empty list.** Previously, creating a list without any filter criteria added every contact in your account to it. To include all your contacts, add or import them explicitly.
- **Route webhook attachments carry a new `disposition` field.** Each attachment now reports whether it was sent as a conventional attachment, as an inline part embedded in the message body, or with no disposition specified, so your integration can tell a genuine file attachment apart from an embedded image. The field is also present when receiving route events over WebSocket.
- **The per-message delivery attempt log now lists the final outcome last** among attempts recorded in the same second, in both the dashboard and the `delivery_attempts` field of API v2 message responses.
- **CSV imports accept files up to 100MB**, with a clear message in the browser if you pick something larger.
- **The account suspension email now names the affected account** and its account ID, and links directly to it in the dashboard — useful if you manage sub-accounts and receive notifications for more than one account.
- **The inbound routes page now shows MX setup guidance**: Routing is not possible unless your (sub)domain's primary MX record is pointing to AhaSend. The dashboard now has an improved setup guide on the routes page telling you to do so before you can setup routes.

#### Improvements

- **Accounts approved for marketing are no longer penalized for sending marketing.** Our automated content review previously treated all marketing content as a policy violation and scored it at maximum regardless of whether the account was permitted to send it. That decision now respects your account's marketing setting.
- **Imports never re-subscribe anyone.** Contacts who have unsubscribed globally are not added to the target list, and existing list memberships — including unsubscribed ones — are left as they are. Rows with an invalid email address are skipped and reported rather than imported.
- **Sending-pool assignment now reflects the whole umbrella for Platform Partners.** A partner account's reputation tier is calculated from the combined volume and spam rate of itself and all of its sub-accounts rather than only its own traffic, and a sub-account inherits its parent's account age and subscription standing. Sub-accounts of established partners are now routed on reputation-appropriate IPs from their first send instead of defaulting to the neutral pool. Sub-accounts are still assessed on their own spam rate for demotion, so one bad sub-account neither drags down the umbrella nor is shielded by it.
- **Images in marketing campaigns load faster on repeat views.** Cached campaign images now tell recipients' email clients that they can be kept for 24 hours, matching what images already got on first load.
- **Links inside notification emails are now visibly styled as links** instead of blending into the body text. This covers account suspended, credit limit warnings, domain DNS problems, and disabled webhook, routing, and S3 integration notices.

#### Bug Fixes

- **Late and retried delivery events now report what actually happened instead of repeating the last known status.** Our mail server can retry an event notification for up to 24 hours. A retried reception, temporary failure, or bounce arriving after a message was already delivered previously re-sent a duplicate `on_delivered`; it now sends the event that actually occurred. A retried non-delivery event on a message you had already opened or clicked used to produce no webhook and now reports itself. A retried delivery event on an already-opened message still sends nothing, and an expired message still produces exactly one `on_transient_error` and one `on_failed`. Payload shapes are unchanged and no new event types were added.
- **Contact Imports no longer overwrite data you didn't include.** A CSV that omits a column, or leaves a cell blank, now leaves the existing first name, last name, and custom attributes untouched, and custom attributes are merged rather than replaced. Previously an email-only CSV would erase names and attributes on every contact it touched.
- **Fixed expired messages permanently showing as "Deferred".** When a message ran out of delivery attempts, the final "expired" event and the last temporary-failure event carried the same one-second timestamp, and whichever arrived first won — so the message could stay "Deferred" indefinitely in the dashboard and API, carrying the wrong bounce classification and never counted as failed. A message's status is now always taken from its terminal outcome regardless of event ordering. Reporting and aggregate statistics were never affected, and neither were campaign recipients.
- **Fixed errors when viewing or listing a webhook scoped to a sending domain that was later deleted**.
- **Fixed inbound route webhooks silently dropping attachments.** Routes with attachment forwarding enabled were discarding images embedded inline in the message body, and files whose sender omitted the content-disposition header — a common pattern for automatically generated CSV reports. Both are now delivered. Expect larger payloads if your route receives mail with embedded images.
- **Fixed the single-message API endpoint omitting inline attachments from its parsed content**; embedded images are now included with their filename, content type, content ID, and contents.
- **Fixed the team member API endpoints returning an all-zeros `user_id`.** Listing members returned the placeholder for every member, and adding a member returned it whenever the invited address did not already have an AhaSend user. The value can now be used directly with the remove-member endpoint.
- **Fixed DKIM records with optional tags failing validation**. If you publish your DKIM record manually and your DNS provider adds or reorders tags such as `h=sha256`, your domain showed DKIM as unconfigured despite a valid record. Validation now checks the required tag values and ignores extra ones, while still rejecting a wrong public key or a malformed tag list.
- **Fixed notification emails never being delivered to team members whose name contains a non-ASCII character**. Credit-limit warnings, webhook, route and domain disabled alerts, suspension notices, API key security alerts, and campaign review notifications were silently dropped for those recipients.
- **Fixed display names in the `Cc` field of a conversational message being mangled when they contained non-ASCII characters**, and an empty `Cc:` header is no longer added to messages sent with no Cc recipients.
- **Fixed adding contacts to a list from the dashboard silently doing nothing**. Selecting contacts and adding them to a list failed to add anyone; it now works, including re-subscribing a contact who had previously unsubscribed from that list.
- **Fixed CSV files exported from Excel as "CSV UTF-8" being rejected as missing an email column**; the hidden marker these files begin with is now handled.
- **Fixed renaming a contact attribute converting every stored value to text**. Renaming a number, true/false, or date attribute could cause segment filters on it to stop matching; values now keep their original type.
- **Fixed editing a contact attribute's display name being rejected as a duplicate when only its capitalization or spacing changed**. It now saves, and does so without rewriting your whole contact book.
- **Security:** fixed two open-redirect issues in the dashboard. A crafted link to the billing checkout-return page could redirect a signed-in user to an external site, and the re-authentication screen could be tricked into the same via a specially encoded referring page. Both now only accept destinations inside the dashboard. No customer data was accessible through either issue, and we have no indication of exploitation.
- **Fixed an explicit cookie rejection being silently overridden.** If you are outside a jurisdiction where a consent banner is legally required and you had rejected cookies, your choice was being reset to "accept all" on each page load. It is now respected. Stale dashboard-only consent cookies are also cleaned up automatically so they cannot override your current choice.
- **Fixed changing your account email address**: entering a different capitalization of your current address is now recognized as no change, and confirming a change to an address another user already holds now shows a clear error instead of appearing to succeed.

<Update label="June 2026">
#### New Features

- **Sub-Accounts**: Platform Partner and reseller accounts can now create and manage sub-accounts under a single parent, from both the dashboard and the v2 API. Each sub-account is a full account — its own domains, sending, webhooks, routes, and members — that is billed through the parent, with usage pooled onto the parent's invoice and a proportional per-sub cost breakdown available in the dashboard and API.
- **API-Only Sub-Account Provisioning**: Partners can provision a new sub-account and mint its first working API key entirely over the API, with no dashboard step. The new key belongs to the sub-account, and its secret is returned only once (or on an exact idempotent retry within five minutes).
- **API Key IP Allow Lists**: Each v2 API key can now be restricted to a set of IPv4/IPv6 addresses or CIDR ranges. When a key has a non-empty allow list, any request made with it from an IP that isn't on the list is rejected on every endpoint, regardless of the key's permissions. An empty list means no restriction, so existing keys are unaffected. Up to 100 entries per key; the "allow everything" ranges are rejected.
- **Per-Domain DKIM Selectors**: Platform Partner accounts can now set a custom DKIM selector for each sending domain — in the dashboard and via the v2 API — instead of only an account-wide selector, so the same domain can be authenticated across multiple senders without collisions. The selector can be changed while the domain's DKIM is unverified and locks once verified (contact support to unlock).

#### Changes

- **API key responses now include an `ip_allow_list` field.** The [list](/api-reference/api-keys/get-api-keys), [get](/api-reference/api-keys/get-api-key), [create](/api-reference/api-keys/create-api-key), and [update](/api-reference/api-keys/update-api-key) API-key endpoints always return the key's allow list (an empty array when the key has no restriction).
- **Changing a key's IP allow list in the dashboard now requires confirming it's you.** Saving an allow-list change prompts for a current two-factor code (or a recovery code) if you have two-factor enabled, otherwise your account password; the confirmation is remembered for five minutes. Dashboard management works from any IP and is never blocked by a key's own allow list.
- **Security alert email for allow-list changes.** When an API key's IP allow list is created or changed, account owners and administrators receive an email noting that a security setting on the named key changed. The email never contains the key's secret or the list contents.
- **The v2 domain API now accepts and returns a `dkim_selector` field.** [Create](/api-reference/domains/create-domain) and [update](/api-reference/domains/update-domain) accept an optional per-domain selector (Platform Partner accounts only; a trimmed-empty value clears it and reverts to the default), and the domain response reports the configured override (null when none is set). The custom tracking, return-path, subscription, and media subdomain fields are now documented as applying to both self-managed and managed DNS.
- **Every v2 account response now includes a `parent_account_id` field**, so you can tell whether an account is a sub-account and which parent it belongs to.
- **The v1 send API now rejects a request with neither a text body nor an HTML body.** Both the transactional send and the send-with-substitutions endpoint return a validation error when both body fields are omitted; at least one must be provided.
- **Suspended accounts can no longer manage sending domains** To prevent abuse, while an account is suspended, adding, editing, deleting, or re-checking DNS for a domain is blocked with a message to contact support.
- **The billing page now shows emails sent "in the current billing cycle"** for customers on a paid subscription, instead of "in the past 30 days."
- **Custom Subdomains on Self-Managed DNS**: Domains that use your own self-published DNS can now customize their tracking, return-path, subscription, and media subdomains, matching what was already available for managed-DNS domains. Each subdomain is editable until its DNS record is verified, then locks.
- **Golang SDK**: The [AhaSend Go SDK](https://github.com/AhaSend/ahasend-go) now supports Sub-Account features and API key IP allow lists.
- **CLI**: The [AhaSend CLI](https://github.com/AhaSend/ahasend-cli) now supports the Sub-Account management features.

#### Improvements

- **Safer API retries.** Retrying a request while the original is still processing now returns a clear "conflict" response with retry guidance, and reusing the same idempotency key on a different endpoint, resource, or payload is rejected instead of replaying the wrong response — so retries no longer risk creating duplicate resources.
- **Self-lockout protection on API-key allow lists.** If you use a key to set its own IP allow list to a value that excludes the IP you're calling from, the change is rejected and nothing is saved. You can still change it from the dashboard, from an already-allowed IP, or with a different key.
- **Sub-account and API-key updates reject empty or no-change requests** with a clear error and leave omitted fields untouched; the website field is optional when updating a sub-account.

#### Bug Fixes

- Fixed legacy plan customers being unable to upgrade to the Pro or Max plan; these upgrades now complete correctly.
- Fixed plan upgrades failing for cards that require extra verification; the upgrade now goes through against your saved card without a failed authentication prompt.
- Fixed a legacy-plan upgrade being blocked for up to 24 hours after you updated your payment method; retrying with a new card now works immediately.
- Fixed backward ("previous page") cursor pagination on v2 list endpoints, which could jump back to the first page instead of the page immediately before your cursor.
- Fixed the profile account switcher so deleted accounts no longer appear and can no longer break the page.

</Update>
<Update label="May 2026">
#### New Features

- **Duplicate a Campaign**: You can now duplicate an existing marketing campaign in one action. The copy includes the design, sender settings, subject, preview text, tags, and recipient lists, and opens as a new editable draft — delivery history and statistics are not carried over.
- **Automatic Tax on Subscriptions**: New paid subscriptions now have applicable sales tax or VAT automatically calculated and added to your invoices based on your billing location.

#### Changes

- **Transactional sends are limited to 100 recipients per request.** The v1 send API now enforces a maximum of 100 recipients per call and rejects empty recipient lists with a validation error, matching the documented v2 limit.
- **Each recipient gets a unique Message-ID.** When you send to multiple recipients via the v1 send API without supplying your own Message-ID, every recipient now receives an individually generated, unique Message-ID instead of a shared one, making each message separately trackable. Supplying your own Message-ID works as before.
- **Higher domain limits for enterprise and reseller accounts.** Enterprise and Platform Partner accounts can now add up to 10,000 sending domains.

#### Improvements

- **Cost estimates now reflect your subscription's currency.** The estimated-amount figure on the billing page is calculated using EUR or GBP pricing for EUR/GBP subscribers, instead of dollar-based rates.
- **Fewer false-positive account suspensions.** Automated bounce-rate protection now measures your actual bounce rate over the trailing 30 days and only acts once you've accumulated a meaningful number of bounces, so low-volume accounts are no longer suspended over a handful of bounces. Reviewed accounts that are reinstated are judged only on their activity going forward.
- **Clearer error when adding a duplicate contact.** Adding a contact whose email already exists now shows a specific "a contact with this email address already exists" message instead of a generic failure.
- **Better partial-failure reporting on multi-recipient sends.** When some recipients in a send fail, the API response now clearly reports which recipients failed and why, rather than failing the entire request opaquely.

#### Bug Fixes

- Fixed credit purchases that could silently fail to complete; credit top-ups are now provisioned reliably.
- Fixed plan upgrades occasionally resolving to the wrong plan when an invoice contained proration credits.
- Fixed the dashboard billing page showing a contradictory "your subscription is about to be cancelled" banner for customers who had scheduled a cancellation and then started a new subscription.
- Fixed the billing and credits pages failing to load for brand-new subscriptions that had not yet produced their first invoice; usage, billing dates, and estimated charges now display correctly in that window.
- Fixed the credits history failing to render entries that had no linked invoice; such entries now show "N/A" and a support contact link.
- Fixed the dashboard notice about an upcoming scheduled plan change to show the correct current and target plans and the right wording (for example, "downgrade" only when it actually is one), and to no longer appear when there is no real pending change. Plan changes made through the Stripe billing portal now reflect correctly on your account.
- Fixed unpaid checkouts falling through without an error message; if a checkout completes without a successful payment, you'll now see a prompt to retry with a different payment method.
- Fixed updated checkout links for purchasing additional credit packs.
- Improved the contrast of the status indicators on the campaign edit page.

</Update>

<Update label="April 2026">
#### New Features

- **Multi-Currency Billing**: New paid subscriptions are now billed in EUR, GBP, or USD depending on your location. The same applies when upgrading or switching between Pro and Max plans. Your default currency is captured when your account is verified, and prices on the onboarding and dashboard billing pages — both plan base prices and per-1k-email overage rates — are now shown in your local currency with the correct symbol (€9 / £8 / $10 for the Pro plan, etc.). You can override the auto-detected currency with a `currency` cookie.
- **Custom Subdomains on Domain Creation**: The API v2 [create-domain endpoint](/api-reference/domains/create-domain) now accepts `tracking_subdomain`, `return_path_subdomain`, `subscription_subdomain`, `media_subdomain`, and `dkim_rotation_interval_days` so domains can be set up with custom subdomains in a single API call.
- **Subdomains lock after DNS verification.** Once a sending-domain subdomain (tracking, return-path, subscription, media) is verified in DNS, it is locked: any change requires contacting support, and the [update domain API](/api-reference/domains/update-domain) returns a clear 400 error indicating which subdomain is locked.
- **API v2 Domain Management**: API clients can now [set](/api-reference/domains/create-domain) and [update](/api-reference/domains/update-domain) domain settings and [trigger DNS re-checks](/api-reference/domains/check-dns), including custom tracking, return-path, subscription, media, and DKIM rotation settings for managed DNS domains.
- **BYODKIM Compatibility**: Platform Partner accounts can now [create domains](/api-reference/domains/create-domain) with 1024-bit BYODKIM keys when needed for legacy compatibility. 2048-bit keys remain recommended.
- **Work Email Required at Signup**: To reduce abuse, new account sign-ups must now use a work email address. Free, personal, and temporary email providers are no longer accepted at registration.

#### Changes

- **Email addresses are normalized on messages and suppressions** (lowercased and trimmed of surrounding whitespace) on write and on lookup, making suppression checks robust to casing and whitespace variations.
- **API v2 message scheduling validation is stricter:** [Create Message API](/api-reference/messages/create-message) scheduled expiration must be in the future and within the documented limit.

#### Improvements

- **Better API v2 error responses.** `403 Forbidden` (not `401`) is returned for insufficient scopes; `400` (not `404`/`412`) for invalid sender domains or DNS-invalid domains on send; clearer errors for invalid suppression domains, invalid webhook boolean filters, and message-status enum mismatches.
- **Idempotent create endpoints document the `422` response** that is returned when an idempotency key is reused with a different payload.
- **API v2 domain-scoped routes, webhooks, SMTP credentials, messages, and statistics** now handle scoped access more consistently.
- **Account updates return the updated account**, not the pre-update copy. The account response no longer leaks internal fields such as suspension status, monthly credit, or contact attributes; it now includes `owner_id`.
- **Account billing pages now keep customer currency consistent** when viewing existing subscriptions or switching plans.
- **Plan cards now show localized overage prices** instead of hardcoded USD amounts.

#### Bug fixes

- **Domain names are normalized everywhere.** Domain names submitted to the API, the dashboard, or as part of an API key scope are now lowercased, trimmed, and stripped of any trailing dot, so equivalent names are no longer treated as different.
- Dashboard links in campaign review, DNS, route, and webhook notification emails now point directly to the correct pages.
- Fixed manually resumed campaigns getting sent back through the normal evaluation gate after an admin resumed them.
- Fixed message cancellation requiring `messages:send` instead of `messages:cancel` permission.
- Fixed message send results being assembled without synchronization, which could occasionally drop or duplicate per-recipient entries.
- Fixed webhook list `enabled` and `on_*` boolean query parameters being silently ignored when given non-boolean values.

- **Managed DNS & DKIM Rotation Setup**: Sending-domain setup now supports our new native managed DNS flow. Managed domains get two DKIM CNAME records: one required for sending and a second recommended record that lets AhaSend rotate DKIM keys later without asking for another DNS change.

- DNS validation now follows CNAME chains, so domains using intermediate CNAME records (e.g., through a CDN or DNS proxy) validate correctly.
- DMARC checking recovers more gracefully from transient DNS failures, preserving the previously known state instead of marking the domain invalid.
- The dashboard adapts better to light and dark color modes.
- Checkout and payment form initialization is more reliable.
- Established accounts (verified, or 30+ days old with significant volume) can now disable message data retention, in addition to approved accounts.

#### Bug Fixes

- Fixed character encoding corruption (mojibake) in campaign emails when HTML entities such as non-breaking spaces were converted to UTF-8 by the tracking system.
- Fixed single-part campaign emails not receiving click or open tracking.
- Fixed API v2 send-only keys not appearing in the list API keys response.
- Fixed domain deletion not cleaning up associated API key scopes.
- Fixed text selection being unreadable in code blocks on the dashboard.
- Fixed checkout validation requiring company name and VAT ID together for Singapore customers, where VAT is not applicable.
