AhaSend verifies domains hosted at STRATO when you publish the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records from your domain page; this guide shows where to enter them.

<DnsPrerequisites />

## Open the DNS Form

Open **Domains → Domainverwaltung**, select the gear beside your domain, then **DNS → TXT- und CNAME-Records**. Choose the type from AhaSend and fill in **Präfix** and **Wert**. These steps follow [STRATO's DNS guide](https://www.strato.de/faq/domains/wie-kann-ich-bei-strato-meine-dns-eintraege-verwalten/).

STRATO adds the domain to Präfix. Its SPF and DMARC settings can supply records automatically: inspect them before adding custom TXT values. To supply your own DMARC record, choose **Keine STRATO-DMARC-Regel** and publish the one policy you intend to use. Preserve authorization for any STRATO mailbox service that still sends mail.

## Add SPF, DKIM and DMARC

For a zone named `example.com`, use this name mapping. The examples show a common managed-DKIM setup; your dashboard's names and targets take precedence.

| AhaSend record | Type | Name in the provider form | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPF for `example.com` | TXT | leave Präfix empty | SPF value shown by AhaSend, merged into any existing SPF record |
| `managed._domainkey.example.com` | CNAME | `managed._domainkey` | First DKIM target from your domain page |
| `managed2._domainkey.example.com` | CNAME | `managed2._domainkey` | Second DKIM target from your domain page |
| `_dmarc.example.com` | TXT | `_dmarc` | Your reviewed DMARC policy |
