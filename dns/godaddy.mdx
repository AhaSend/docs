AhaSend verifies domains hosted at GoDaddy when you publish the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records from your domain page; this guide shows where to enter them.

<DnsPrerequisites />

## Open the DNS Form

Open your Domain Portfolio, select the domain, then **DNS → Add New Record**. Choose the type from AhaSend and fill in **Name** and **Value**. These steps follow [GoDaddy's DNS guide](https://www.godaddy.com/help/add-a-txt-record-19232).

Enter only the prefix in Name: `managed._domainkey`, not `managed._domainkey.example.com`. Save with **Save**, or **Save All Records** if you added several rows. If DNS editing is unavailable, check whether another provider hosts the domain’s nameservers.

## Add SPF, DKIM and DMARC

For a zone named `example.com`, use this name mapping. The examples show a common managed-DKIM setup; your dashboard's names and targets take precedence.

| AhaSend record | Type | Name in the provider form | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPF for `example.com` | TXT | `@` | SPF value shown by AhaSend, merged into any existing SPF record |
| `managed._domainkey.example.com` | CNAME | `managed._domainkey` | First DKIM target from your domain page |
| `managed2._domainkey.example.com` | CNAME | `managed2._domainkey` | Second DKIM target from your domain page |
| `_dmarc.example.com` | TXT | `_dmarc` | Your reviewed DMARC policy |
