AhaSend verifies domains hosted at Namecheap when you publish the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records from your domain page; this guide shows where to enter them.

<DnsPrerequisites />

## Open the DNS Form

Open **Domain List → Manage → Advanced DNS**, then use **Host Records → Add New Record**. Choose the type from AhaSend and fill in **Host** and **Value**. These steps follow [Namecheap's DNS guide](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/317/2237/how-do-i-add-txtspfdkimdmarc-records-for-my-domain/).

Use **TXT Record** for SPF and DMARC. If the Mail Settings area already manages an SPF record, edit that SPF setup rather than adding a second SPF record in Host Records. Custom nameservers mean you need to make the change at that other DNS host.

## Add SPF, DKIM and DMARC

For a zone named `example.com`, use this name mapping. The examples show a common managed-DKIM setup; your dashboard's names and targets take precedence.

| AhaSend record | Type | Name in the provider form | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPF for `example.com` | TXT | `@` | SPF value shown by AhaSend, merged into any existing SPF record |
| `managed._domainkey.example.com` | CNAME | `managed._domainkey` | First DKIM target from your domain page |
| `managed2._domainkey.example.com` | CNAME | `managed2._domainkey` | Second DKIM target from your domain page |
| `_dmarc.example.com` | TXT | `_dmarc` | Your reviewed DMARC policy |
