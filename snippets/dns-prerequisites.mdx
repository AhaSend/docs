## Before You Start

[Add the sending domain in AhaSend](/domains) and keep its DNS record list open. Sign in to the account that can edit the domain’s authoritative DNS: the provider named by its nameservers. Buying a domain from one company does not always mean it hosts the DNS.

Copy your current records before editing. Keep existing website, mailbox and verification records. See [domain setup](/domains) for the exact AhaSend values, required records and verification timing.
